About

Gonzalo Muñoz García

Business Analytics & Business Administration graduate, currently completing ATPL(A) commercial pilot training. With professional experience across financial controlling, insurance analytics and business intelligence, I have developed strong expertise in Power BI, Python, SQL and Excel — translating complex datasets into structured, actionable insights.

The aviation industry generates vast amounts of operational data — delay patterns, traffic flows, emissions, airspace performance — yet this data rarely reaches decision-makers in a clear, meaningful format. My goal is to bridge that gap, combining an analytical background with a deep understanding of aviation operations.

DataPilot brings both disciplines together: a portfolio of aviation analytics projects, industry news and data-driven insights at the intersection of data science and aerospace.
